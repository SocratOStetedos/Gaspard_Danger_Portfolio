Beyond the code

The things I do outside work and school, and what they have taught me about discipline, teamwork and resilience.

Travel and new cultures

I have travelled to Spain, Italy, Austria, the Czech Republic and Slovakia. I have not yet been to the Americas, Asia or Africa, and I would like my international internship to take me somewhere completely new.

Strength training

I started two years ago in Aigrefeuille-sur-Maine with the Club Alpha sports association, and I now train in Lannion after classes. Regular sessions train my body and my mind, and they force me to organise my days.

Running

I run in nature, which builds endurance and lets me take on personal challenges. Steady effort also helps me manage stress, in my studies as much as in sport.

Football

Two years at FCCV (Football Club Coteaux du Vignoble) taught me to communicate, adapt quickly to the game and handle collective pressure. Those habits carry straight over to team projects.

Chess

Chess taught me logic, anticipation and patience: read the position, foresee the opponent's moves and manage time under pressure. It is close to how I approach debugging and security problems.

Volunteering with IFAC

I volunteered with IFAC, an association supporting young people in Loire-Atlantique, preparing evening events and running activities at the youth centre in Château-Thébaud. It taught me communication, organisation and group leadership.

Web development

I like building small tools in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, from games to network simulators. They are listed on the Projects page.
